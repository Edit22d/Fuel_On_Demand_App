Two sibling services share a single integration contract rather than a shared codebase: the backend exposes REST + Socket.IO at port 8000 under `/api/v1`, and the Flutter frontend targets that same surface. The contract (error envelope `message`/`errors`, OTP 120 s / resend 30 s, `+256` phone normalisation, payment aliases `mtn`/`airtel`/`wallet`/`cash`, station `prices` map) is documented in both READMEs so each side can evolve independently. The Flutter app currently uses local mocks (`shared_preferences`) per screen, with a planned one-by-one migration to the real API; the backend's Swagger (`/docs`) serves as the living spec for that migration.
